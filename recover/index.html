Restore your NSD-S3 signer

NSD-S3 is a hardware Nostr signer built on the LilyGo T-Display S3 (ESP32-S3, 1.9" display, native USB) — your private key lives on the board itself and never touches a computer. This page is for a completely fresh / wiped board. After connecting you'll need to tap RESTORE on the device itself before it accepts anything — then enter your 12-word backup here with search-as-you-type; the device derives the key and never sends it back over serial.

Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop only (WebSerial). If the device already has a key, you'll be offered the chance to erase it here first — only do that if you meant to overwrite it; use the on-device REVEAL flow instead to check backups of an existing key.
Your 12 words pass through this browser tab's memory while you type them (same one-time trust as the on-device serial /restore-words command they call). Only do this on a computer you trust, and only for words you're deliberately restoring right now — never leave this page open afterward.

Wipes just the key — the device stays fully working, no reflash needed.

Alternatively, a full reflash at flash.flink.club also erases the key (along with everything else) — only useful if the firmware itself needs reinstalling.

Confirm on the device

The device is now showing ERASE?. Hold the button next to HOLD ERASE > for 3 seconds to actually wipe the key — or tap CANCEL > on the device to back out.

Waiting for the device…

Press RESTORE on the device

The device won't accept words until you confirm this in person. On its screen, tap the button next to RESTORE > — you have 5 minutes once you do.

Waiting for the device…

Enter your 12 backup words

Tap a word box to select it, then spell it out on the on-screen keyboard below — the letters shuffle to a new layout for each word, so a keylogger or shoulder-surfer can't build a map of key positions across your whole backup. Matching suggestions appear as you go; click one to confirm it and move to the next word. All 12 must be exact, valid BIP-39 words before Restore unlocks.

Now set a PIN on the device

Words accepted. The device is showing SET PIN on its screen — choose a 6-digit PIN using its own buttons (BOTTOM cycles 0–9, TOP confirms each digit), then enter the same PIN again to confirm. Your PIN never touches this page.

Waiting for the device…
Restore complete — the device now has a key.
