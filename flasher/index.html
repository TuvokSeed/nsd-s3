Flash your nostr-signer-s3

For the LilyGo T-Display S3 hardware Nostr signer used with flink.club and the LNbits-NSD-compatible extensions (horse, nos2x-nsd).

Works in Chrome or Edge on desktop only — flashing uses the WebSerial API, which Safari and Firefox don't support. Not available on mobile browsers.
Your browser doesn't support WebSerial — open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be served over HTTPS to flash a device.

Before you plug in

  • Use a USB-C cable that carries data (not a charge-only cable).
  • Click Connect device & install, then pick the board from the Chrome port picker.
  • Flashing from this page erases the whole device, including your key vault. Make sure your 12-word backup is written down before you flash — afterwards the device starts fresh (new key, or restore).
  • Have your 12-word backup? Restore it at recover.flink.club once flashing finishes, then set a new PIN on the device.

First flash on a factory board

If the board has never run this firmware, you may need to hold the BOOT button while plugging in the USB cable (this forces the ROM bootloader), then press reset once flashing finishes.
